# Notice of suspected unauthorized use of project concepts

**Status:** Open investigation  
**Project:** ATOM / Sentry / related Nova project family  
**Purpose:** Preserve a public, timestamped record while the provenance review is ongoing.

## Statement

We have identified similarities between material developed within our ATOM / Sentry project family and material appearing in later or separately published AI-assistant concepts.

At this stage, **we suspect that parts of our ideas, concepts, design direction, or technical approach may have been used without authorization**. This is an allegation under investigation, not a finding of copying, infringement, or misconduct.

We are intentionally **not identifying any person, designer, company, account, or other suspected party at this stage**.

## What we are investigating

The review includes possible unauthorized use or derivation of elements relating to:

- the ATOM / Sentry product concept and naming architecture;
- an AI-agent family built around a shared technical foundation;
- specialized assistants/agents for different tasks and users;
- cockpit-style agent interaction and visible task/status progression;
- the structure and visual direction of the assistant UI/UX;
- persistent/continuous agent operation and background work;
- memory and controlled learning concepts;
- browser/research/tool orchestration;
- model/provider routing and approval boundaries;
- child, teen and adult variants of the assistant experience;
- the broader use of **Atom / Nova** naming and related project-family concepts where the combination overlaps with other distinctive project elements.

## Evidence standard

A shared name, dark interface, chat screen, AI assistant, or agent architecture is **not by itself evidence of copying**. Many of these ideas exist independently across the AI industry.

Our investigation therefore focuses on combinations of elements, chronology, repository history, original assets, technical descriptions, commits, publication dates, and—where evidence exists—possible access to earlier project material.

## Existing project record

The public ATOM/Sentry repository contains project descriptions and assets documenting, among other things:

- a continuous agent loop and background work;
- priority/resource management;
- goal graphs, dependencies and blocker states;
- browser and research-tool orchestration;
- memory and controlled learning;
- PWA UI;
- provider/model-routing foundations;
- logging and approval/safety boundaries;
- a cockpit UI combining conversation, active tasks, projects, tools, browser, files, memory and results;
- separate adult, teen and child interface directions;
- a child-assistant principle of helping the child solve tasks rather than simply doing the work for them.

Repository history and original source assets will be used to establish chronology.

## What this notice does NOT claim

This notice does **not** claim that any currently unnamed person or organization has committed copyright infringement, theft, trade-secret misuse, or any other unlawful act.

It records that we have a genuine concern and are preserving and comparing evidence.

## Next steps

We are assembling a chronology based on Git commits, original files/assets, publication timestamps and archived public material. Future updates to this document should distinguish clearly between:

1. independently verifiable facts;
2. observed similarities;
3. evidence concerning chronology or access; and
4. conclusions that can actually be supported by that evidence.

No suspected party will be publicly identified here unless the evidence has first been reviewed carefully and there is a sound factual basis for doing so.
